Get to Know Me

I am a software engineering graduate with a strong interest in building systems that are clear, maintainable, and intentional.

I approach learning the same way I approach software: start simple, understand the fundamentals, and improve through deliberate iteration.

Outside of coding, I'm fascinated by fintech innovations, enjoy experimenting with automation, and love exploring how technology can solve everyday problems. I also have a soft spot for music, reading sci-fi, and tinkering with small side projects that challenge me to learn something new every day.

My Journey So Far

My path into software engineering hasn't been linear. It's been shaped by curiosity, frustration, rebuilding, and learning to slow down.

  1. Early Curiosity

    My interest in technology started with curiosity — wanting to understand how applications and websites actually work.

  2. Learning the Tools Without Owning the Thinking

    I gained exposure to core programming concepts, development tools, and structured problem solving approaches.

    I could build things that worked, but I often followed patterns without fully understanding 'why' they worked.

  3. Shipping My First Project

    I built and shipped my first project, which was a huge milestone. It was exciting to see something I built come to life, but I also realized there were many areas where I could improve my understanding and approach.

    It exposed gaps in my fundamentals, and I knew I needed to go back and learn more deeply.

  4. Present Focus

    I am currently focused on strengthening fundamentals, documenting my thinking, and building projects that can scale in complexity over time.

How I Think
